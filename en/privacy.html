PRIVACY NOTICE

A voyage saved
on your device

Updated October 1, 2026. Applies to Tide Paws / 밀물냥 1.0.0, currently preparing for release.

Information stored on your device

Tide Paws is an offline puzzle game with no account sign-up. Completed puzzles, best records, the current board, daily puzzle records and language, sound and haptics preferences are stored on your device. The app does not send these game records, your name, contact information or location to a developer server.

The first public version contains no ads, in-app purchases, external analytics or tracking SDKs.

Sharing and exports you choose

When you choose to share a result, the device share feature can pass a PNG image containing the actual puzzle date, moves, stars and game name to an app you select. That app or service processes the content under its own policy. Temporary image files are stored in the app's device cache.

Playtest notes are off by default. Turning them on in Settings keeps up to 200 recent puzzle events and dates in memory for the current app session. Turning notes off or ending the app session clears this event list. Choosing Export lets you save or share a JSON file. It contains no account or device identifier, contact information or location. There is no automatic submission or server upload.

Erasing and restoring records

The erase-progress action in Settings removes this game's progress and preferences. The app cannot undo that deletion. It does not provide account-based recovery or sync between devices. Operating-system backups and uninstall behavior depend on your device settings and platform policies.

Support and platform services

Contact details and messages you voluntarily send to the support email are used to investigate and answer your request. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information. You can use the same address for requests about support correspondence.

App stores and operating systems may process installation and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. The app does not use a separate analytics SDK to transmit that information to the developer.
