Guide cats in swim rings to a welcoming dock in Tide Paws.

How do I play?

Swipe the sea up, down, left or right. Every cat moves in the same direction, stops at a rock or another cat, and is rescued when it passes through a dock. Bring everyone home to finish the puzzle.

You can use the arrow buttons instead of swiping. Undo returns to the previous move; A little hint suggests a direction. There is no timer.

Where is my progress?

Completed puzzles, best records, the current board and settings stay on the device you play on. There is no account or sync between devices. When you reopen the app, use Continue on the home screen to resume your puzzle.

Erasing progress or reinstalling the app can remove your records. If the app says progress could not be saved, keep the current app session open and contact support.
